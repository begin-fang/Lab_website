About SAMER Lab

The Space Autonomous Manipulation and Exploration Robotics (SAMER) Lab is dedicated to developing cutting-edge robotic systems for space applications. Our research focuses on autonomous manipulation, mobility, and exploration technologies that enable robots to operate effectively in the challenging environments of space.

Space Servicing & Manipulation

Developing robotic systems for in-orbit satellite servicing, assembly, and maintenance. Our technologies enable autonomous refueling, repair, and debris removal operations that extend mission lifetimes and improve space sustainability.

  • Autonomous satellite servicing
  • On-orbit assembly of large structures
  • Space debris mitigation

Planetary Exploration & Detection

Creating autonomous robotic systems for planetary surface exploration, capable of operating in extreme environments from the Moon to Mars and beyond. Our rovers and landers incorporate advanced autonomy for scientific discovery.

  • Autonomous navigation in unknown terrain
  • Sample collection and analysis
  • Multi-robot coordination for exploration

Research Areas

Exploring the frontiers of autonomous space robotics

Autonomous Manipulation

Developing advanced algorithms for robotic arms to perform complex tasks in space without human intervention, including assembly, maintenance, and sample collection.

Planetary Exploration

Creating robust robotic systems capable of autonomous navigation and scientific investigation on planetary surfaces, from Mars to icy moons.

AI for Space Robotics

Implementing machine learning and computer vision techniques to enable robots to make intelligent decisions in unpredictable space environments.

Orbital Servicing

Designing robotic systems for in-orbit satellite servicing, including refueling, repair, and debris removal operations.

Extreme Environment Robotics

Developing robots that can withstand and operate in the extreme temperatures, radiation, and vacuum conditions of space.

Multi-Robot Systems

Investigating coordination strategies for teams of robots working together to accomplish complex space missions more efficiently.

Recent Publications

Our contributions to the field of space robotics

"Adaptive Grasping for Irregular Objects in Microgravity"

Journal of Space Robotics, 2023

Presented a novel grasping algorithm that enables robotic arms to securely manipulate irregularly shaped objects in microgravity conditions with 98% success rate.

Authors: Johnson, S., Rodriguez, E., Chen, M. Read Paper

"Autonomous Navigation for Planetary Rovers Using Deep Reinforcement Learning"

IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems, 2023

Developed a DRL-based navigation system that reduces energy consumption by 40% while maintaining high path-finding accuracy in unknown Martian terrain.

Authors: Chen, M., Patel, P., Rodriguez, E. Read Paper

"Multi-Agent Coordination for In-Space Assembly of Large Structures"

Acta Astronautica, 2022

Proposed a decentralized control framework for teams of robots to collaboratively assemble large space structures with minimal communication overhead.

Authors: Wong, A., Garcia, M., Rodriguez, E. Read Paper

"Resilient Control Systems for Space Robotics Under Communication Delay"

AIAA Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics, 2022

Introduced a novel control architecture that maintains operational capability even with significant Earth-to-space communication delays.

Authors: Rodriguez, E., Kim, D., Wilson, J. Read Paper
